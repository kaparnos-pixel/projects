The port call is the least-digitised step in global trade

Every voyage ends in a port call that must be operationally and financially executed — yet the workflow is fragmented and opaque. The recurring failure points:

📄

Disbursement disputes

Pro-forma and final DAs circulate as loose attachments, challenged weeks later with no shared record.

🧭

Fragmented sourcing

Finding a vetted supplier in an unfamiliar port means cold outreach — slow, opaque, impossible to compare.

🗂️

No audit trail

“Who agreed to what, and when?” is scattered across personal inboxes and lost when staff leave.

🛡️

Compliance & fraud risk

Cross-border maritime payments carry real sanctions and fraud exposure, handled with limited visibility.

One platform, organised around the port call

A port call has two distinct sides — the appointment of an agent and the procurement of supplies. BEACON splits them so each has the right workflow, then connects them so data is never re-keyed.
