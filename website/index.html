One hub between the owner and the world's ports

AusGlobal is the HUB agent. It signs a master agreement with the principal, then outsources local execution to pre-vetted sub-agents under strict SLAs, taking full responsibility for the result.

Revenue model

How AusGlobal earns

The principal pays the full invoice to the hub; the hub passes the bulk to the sub-agent and keeps a service margin.

  • Principal pays the full invoice to the hub
  • Hub remits ~90% to the sub-agent
  • Agency fee & husbandry handling on top

Sub-agents are sourced for their local authority relationships, then put through due diligence on financial stability, anti-corruption (TRACE / FCPA alignment) and ISO 9001 before signing the SLA and frame agreement.

Every port call runs end to end on one platform

When a vessel schedules a call, the integrated operational and financial workflow triggers between principal, hub and sub-agent — each with their own login, dashboard and platform email address. The system auto-generates an email on every input.

📝

1 · Appointment & PDA

Principal appoints AusGlobal; the hub forwards to the local sub-agent, who submits a Proforma DA in a standardised format. The hub vets it against agreed tariffs and sends it for approval.

🏦

2 · Funding & pre-funding

The principal approves and transfers 100% of the estimate to the central hub account. The hub holds the capital and releases matching advances to the sub-agent — never the full sum up front.

⚓

3 · Port-call execution

The sub-agent handles husbanding — customs, pilotage, bunkering, provisioning — and logs the Statement of Facts live: ETA, NOR tendered, all fast, cargo commenced and completed, unberthed.

🧾

4 · FDA, audit & settlement

The sub-agent files the Final DA with vouchers; hub auditors review line-by-line; AusGlobal issues one unified invoice and refunds or credits any unused balance to the next voyage.
